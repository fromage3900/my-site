## Preferred five-piece attachment / link story

1. one clean material-viewer hero;
2. one grazing-angle or channel-inspection proof;
3. one genuine CLO garment/source proof;
4. one Blender/web-prep or GLB proof;
5. concise pipeline/evaluation sheet.

Do not flood the client with every Melodia material experiment.

## Figma packaging rule

Figma is presentation only. It may improve hierarchy, spacing, annotation and consistency; it may not:
- invent runtime metrics;
- remove provenance caveats;
- imply measured textile physics;
- fabricate client results;
- hide that a state is still unverified.

## Submit gate

The fabric consultant application may be human-submitted when:
- the existing viewer has a clean runtime smoke test;
- every shown texture/material is commercially safe or clearly presented only as architecture/process evidence;
- at least one genuine Brennan-owned CLO or Blender artifact is selected;
- application claims match observed evidence;
- listing is rechecked at send time.

A full knitwear configurator is **not** a prerequisite for this consulting listing.
