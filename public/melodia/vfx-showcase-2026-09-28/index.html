Hybrid Render Pipeline Architecture

UE5 Niagara + Materials → SceneCapture / Cam → Spout Bridge → TD PostFX Chain → Nikki Bloom + LUT + Vignette → Audio-Reactive Overlay → MovieFileOut / Spout

Each render flows through the full Melodia real-time pipeline: Unreal Engine 5 beauty passes are streamed via Spout (syphonspoutoutTOP: TD_Portfolio_Output) into TouchDesigner's postfx chain — a 3-pass Gaussian bloom, Nikki LUT colour grade, radial vignette, and nebula background composite. Audio CHOPs (5-band spectral analysis) drive Melusina ring transforms and BeatPulse particle emission. Final composite outputs to both moviefileoutTOP for offline capture and spoutout1 for live preview.
