Editorial Feature

The First Dream: A Vertical Slice

Melodia's 'First Dream' is a highly concentrated Persona-lite JRPG loop that serves as our foundational vertical slice, bringing together robust subsystems into a seamless player experience.

  • QuillScript Narrative Engine: Empowers character interactions with typed terminal results and strict narrative authority.
  • Rhythm Combat System: Powered by the Harmonix music clock, syncing actions to a 128 BPM highway to defer damage multipliers based on timing.
  • 12 P0 Foundation Gates: The critical baseline matrix ensuring battle UI, save/load persistence, input debouncing, and script routing are fundamentally stable.
  • Echo Multi-Modal Pipeline: The enterprise backbone connecting Blender 5.2, Unreal Engine 5.8, TouchDesigner, and Monolith MCP over specialized IPC ports for a rapid, iterative design workflow.

This vertical slice proves our core JRPG technology and paves the way for the broader environment-art platform's procedural generation and variant automation.
