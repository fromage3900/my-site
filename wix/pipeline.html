Blender 5.2 Melodia Studio & Procedural Generators

Melodia Studio architecture featuring 165 procedural geometry generators across 12 stack categories. IK retargeting pipelines translate optical motion capture datasets seamlessly onto hero character skeletal hierarchies.

Automated Material Crosswalks & Toon Math

Converts sRGB color space to Linear via standard transformation:
Linear(c) = c / 12.92 for c ≤ 0.04045, else ((c + 0.055) / 1.055)^2.4.
Warm-Violet Evaluation: Evaluated Linear Vector: LinearColor(0.035133, 0.026330, 0.052814, 1.0).
Substrate toon shading profiles configure DiffuseRamp, SpecularRamp, indirect intensity (0.3), extinction (0.3), and stylized hatching textures.

TouchDesigner 4-Band RMS Audio Streamer

Real-time CHOP audio analysis pipeline:
1. Sub-Bass (20–100Hz): Triggers camera shake & low-frequency Niagara ground pulses.
2. Mid-Range (250–2500Hz): Modulates vocal/instrumental lighting & rim light brightness.
3. High-Frequency (4k–12kHz): Drives particle sparkle emitters & rhythm highway highlights.
4. Master RMS Envelope: Controls global atmospheric pulse & post-process bloom.

VOICEVOX & Neural Speech Synthesis

Dual REST speech synthesis pipeline leveraging VOICEVOX with 7 Japanese character voice models alongside custom voice proxies for automated dialogue rendering and lip-sync metadata generation.

Echo Multi-Modal 7-Stage DCC Data Pipeline

Data execution pathway from Blender 5.2 asset authoring to web product compilation and live-ops deployment.

01
Procedural Geometry Authoring: Author procedural curves, architectural ornaments, and environment geometry across 12 stack categories with 165 parametric generators.
02
Engine Automation & Scene Assembly: Execute automated level construction, camera staging, and asset placement via Unreal Engine C++ automation bridges.
03
Blueprint Graph Synthesis: Programmatically generate and compile length-prefixed Blueprint graphs, state machines, and property bindings dynamically.
04
Toon Profile Math & Linear Color Transformation: Configure Substrate toon profile ramps with exact Linear color space evaluations for consistent multi-DCC lighting.
05
TouchDesigner Real-Time Audio Streaming: Broadcast 4-band audio analysis (Sub-Bass, Mid-Range, High-Freq, Master RMS) driving responsive Niagara VFX and post-process bloom.
06
Neural Dialogue Synthesis: Generate Japanese character voice audio and timing phoneme lip-sync metadata via neural speech services.
07
Multi-Platform Product Packaging: Compile master 3D render outputs into 135 emotes, 7 stream overlays, 78 wallpapers, and 12 print-ready postcards.
