Melodia Melusina · Environment Design

Five worlds, one musical journey.

The House, KaleidoNave, Poitou, Sea Above, and Faraway Mother form Melodia's current playable campaign. Each environment pairs authored atmosphere with materials, modular structure, and procedural worldbuilding.

December 25, 2026 prototype · Campaign order

The five explorable chapters.

House → KaleidoNave → Poitou → Sea Above → Faraway Mother. Unlocked Orrery Map waypoints allow revisiting earlier places. Existing image studies are distinguished from final level proof; no unverified in-game captures are substituted here.

01
01 · Home and playable hub

House

Melusina's first explorable sanctuary, with a landscaped setting and domestic arrangement systems that introduce the world at a human scale.

Current campaign chapter; newly approved environment imagery has not been added to the portfolio yet.

02 · Prismatic architecture

KaleidoNave

Ornamental mouldings, clustered columns, faceted masonry, and stained light define a cathedral-scale dream architecture.

See the existing architectural study (look-development evidence, not a full gameplay capture).

03
03 · French regional landscape

Poitou

Stone houses, waterways, regional vegetation, and lived-in architecture create a grounded counterpoint to the surreal worlds. Earlier Melusina Morning artwork is related design research, not finished Poitou imagery.

04
04 · Impossible ocean ecology

Sea Above

Suspended water, resonance and cymatic forms, unusual fauna, and the Horizon Eater shape a surreal world where landscape and music interact.

Optional browser concept study (not an Unreal gameplay capture).

05 · Couture-cymatic architecture

Faraway Mother

Textile-inspired terrain, Cathedral V2, artisan village structures, and the Heart Gate, assembled as an architectural and procedural Unreal environment.

The most complete publicly documented world, with nine reviewed portfolio plates and a material-lookdev comparison.
