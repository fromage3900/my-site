Worlds in Resonance.

An architectural journey through Melodia Melusina. These authored contact sheets document current look development at landscape, architectural and ornamental scales. They are not final Unreal Engine gameplay captures. The existing Faraway Mother Unreal study remains a separate case study; Sea Above and final runtime photographs will be added when reviewed.

I · Seaside House · Ratified Karma XPU Lookdev

Pearl & porcelain.

Vendée coastal terrain, domed roof forms, a shell-like portico and gilded salon interiors. The sheet moves from the coastal panorama through the terrace to the ornament and stair details.

II · KaleidoNave · Ratified Karma XPU Lookdev

Light & resonance.

Floating prismatic architecture, rose-window interiors, scaffold lanterns and an octagonal stage sit within a gorge landscape. The cloud-sea panorama is supporting terrain and volumetric lookdev.

Water & heritage.
