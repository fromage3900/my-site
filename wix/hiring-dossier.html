Short Cover Note

Why I am applying to Infold.

A concise note for the 2027 Art & Visual Design application.

I am a final-year Humber 3D Animation student in Toronto focused on environment art and technical art for stylized games. I am applying to Infold because I am especially interested in worlds where atmosphere, ornament, character identity, and exploration all contribute to the same visual language.

My current project, Melodia Melusina, is a romantic fantasy world built in Unreal Engine 5.8. I create the environments and visual direction while also building reusable material, foliage, PCG, Houdini, and Python workflows underneath them. The part I care about most is still the authored image: composition, color, shape, mood, and the small details that make a place feel worth exploring.

I would be excited to bring that mix of environment craft, stylized worldbuilding, and technical curiosity to an Art & Visual Design team, while learning from artists working at the scale and polish of Infold's games.
