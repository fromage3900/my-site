Short Cover Note

What I am looking for.

A short note that can travel with the current portfolio without being tied to one application.

I am a final-year Humber 3D Animation student in Toronto focused on environment art and technical art for stylized games. I am especially interested in teams where atmosphere, ornament, character identity, and exploration all contribute to the same visual language.

My current project, Melodia Melusina, is a romantic fantasy world built in Unreal Engine 5.8. I create the environments and visual direction while also building reusable material, foliage, PCG, Houdini, and Python workflows underneath them. The part I care about most is still the authored image: composition, color, shape, mood, and the small details that make a place feel worth exploring.

I am looking for environment or technical-art work where I can bring that mix of authored worldbuilding and systems thinking while continuing to learn from a production team.
