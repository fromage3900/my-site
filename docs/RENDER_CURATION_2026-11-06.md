## Product and provenance boundary

MelodiaTouchDesigner source documents Blender riverbed geometry, aquatic flora and MELODIA meadow scatter, including MEL_melodia_meadow_scatter. The five existing River Sanctuary renders are appropriate examples of a procedural botanical environment, but this does NOT prove the visible grass or flowers come from the specific commercial blooms.py / Resonant Blooms release. A paid product cover requires actual direct Blender pack renders.
